How to move

  • Use the mouse to click a link.
  • Use Up and Down, or J and K, to choose a link.
  • Press Enter, Right, or L to open it.
  • Press Left or H to go back.
  • Press Space to read the next part.
  • Press G to type a web address.
  • Press Q to close Lynx.
